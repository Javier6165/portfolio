## P1 — Major

1. **References over-invests in evidence that does not exist yet.** The section uses a flagship-scale heading and a `43rem` interactive ledger although every record is pending. The honesty is excellent, but the prominence creates the page's clearest emotional valley. Collapse it to a compact integrity statement or narrow ledger until at least one recommendation is verified.

2. **The middle of Home is long and structurally repetitive.** Three large Work rows followed by Product practice, AI and References repeatedly use monumental copy beside dense product-UI framing. The craft remains high, but the page gradually drifts from “calm editorial dossier” back toward an app/dashboard aesthetic. Keep Atlas dominant, compact Northstar/Pulse and flatten at least one later artefact with rules and open space instead of nested containers.

3. **Mobile evidence reflows, but does not fully recompose.** There is no horizontal overflow and reading order is correct, yet project UI, Product practice and AI retain many `.58–.7rem` labels inside narrow canvases. They become attractive thumbnails rather than inspectable proof. Mobile needs decisive crops and one enlarged proof point per artefact, not a scaled desktop viewer.

4. **The immediate hero edit settles too slowly.** The cursor handoff now happens within one frame of Presentation mode, which fixes the dead gap. But the full name → role → typo → positioning sequence can extend well beyond the promised four-second opening at normal human key timings. Preserve all three alternatives and the human correction, but consider a single 3–4 second post-Present beat with shorter pauses and a faster final line.
